Who is Jannix Shi?

I am a Senior Software Engineer and Product Creator specializing in Android, Flutter, and iOS development. I am driven by the philosophy that technology should be an invisible assistant—powerful enough to solve complex problems, yet simple enough to be used by anyone.

With over several years of experience in mobile architecture and a keen interest in AI, I spend my time exploring the intersection of high-performance utility and user-centric technology.

"My mission is to build digital tools that don't just function, but delight."

The Vision Behind OmniHub

OmniHub is my most ambitious project to date. It was born from a personal frustration: the "app fatigue" caused by having dozens of single-purpose utilities cluttering my device.

I envisioned a universal toolbox—a single, high-performance application that combines essential utilities like high-precision calculators, lifestyle gadgets, and developer tools, all supercharged by modern AI (Google Gemini).

By integrating advanced OCR and face detection, OmniHub turns your smartphone into a truly "smart" instrument capable of interacting with the physical world in ways traditional apps cannot.
