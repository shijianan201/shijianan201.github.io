# OmniHub Data Deletion Request

At OmniHub, we value your privacy and provide a straightforward way for you to request the deletion of your account and any associated data stored on our systems (specifically Firebase Authentication data).

### How to Request Data Deletion

To delete your account and associated data, please send an email to our support team. We will process your request within **7 business days**.

**Contact Email:** [xiaojiangagaga@gmail.com](mailto:xiaojiangagaga@gmail.com?subject=APP-Omnihub:%20Data%20Deletion%20Request&body=Please%20delete%20my%20OmniHub%20account%20data.%0A%0AUser%20ID%20or%20Registered%20Email:%20[Your%20Email%20Here]%0A%0AConfirmation:%0A[X]%20I%20understand%20that%20this%20action%20is%20permanent%20and%20cannot%20be%20undone.)

---

### Request Details

To ensure your request is categorized and handled correctly, please use the following format:

*   **Subject:** `APP-Omnihub: Data Deletion Request`
*   **Body Content:**
    1.  Your registered email address or User ID (found in App Settings).
    2.  A clear statement that you wish to delete your account and all associated data.
    3.  A confirmation that you understand this action is permanent.

### What Data Will Be Deleted?
Upon processing your request, we will permanently remove:
*   Your Firebase Authentication profile (Email, UID, Provider Info).
*   Any cloud-synced preferences or metadata linked to your account.
*   **Note:** Locally stored data (like your encrypted Password Book) must be deleted by uninstalling the app from your device.

# OmniHub 数据删除请求 (简体中文)

在 OmniHub，我们非常重视您的隐私。如果您希望删除您的账户及存储在我们系统中的相关数据（主要为 Firebase 登录数据），请按照以下说明操作。

### 如何申请删除数据

请通过您的注册邮箱发送邮件至我们的技术支持团队，我们将在 **7 个工作日** 内完成处理。

**联系邮箱:** [xiaojiangagaga@gmail.com](mailto:xiaojiangagaga@gmail.com?subject=APP-Omnihub:%20Data%20Deletion%20Request&body=申请删除%20OmniHub%20登录账户及相关数据。%0A%0A注册邮箱/用户ID:%20[在此填写]%0A%0A确认事项:%0A[X]%20我已知晓此操作不可逆，账户一旦删除将无法恢复。)

### 邮件格式要求
为确保您的请求被快速识别并处理，请务必使用以下格式：

*   **邮件主题:** `APP-Omnihub: Data Deletion Request`
*   **邮件正文:**
    1.  您的注册邮箱或用户 ID（可在应用设置中查看）。
    2.  明确说明您申请删除账户及所有关联数据。

处理您的请求后，我们将永久删除：
*   您的 Firebase 身份验证配置文件（邮箱、UID、登录平台信息）。

*   **注意:** 本地存储的数据（如加密的密码本）需要您通过卸载应用来手动清除。
