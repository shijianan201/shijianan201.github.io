# Privacy Policy for kuntower (Chikun Tower)

**Last Updated: 2026-09-16**

This Privacy Policy describes how kuntower ("Chikun Tower", "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and discloses your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

---

### Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

**1. Information You Provide**
-   **Game Save & Progress Data**: All game progress, floor levels reached, inventory items, player attributes, achievements, and game preferences are stored **locally** on your device by default. If you choose to use the **Google Play Games Cloud Backup** feature, your game save slots and progress metadata can be backed up to and restored from your personal Google Play Games cloud storage. We do not host or store your personal save files on our own external servers.
-   **Feedback and Support**: If you contact us for support or provide feedback, we may collect your name, email address, and the content of your message.

**2. Information Collected Automatically**
-   **In-App Purchases & Financial Data**: We offer optional in-app purchases (such as "Remove Ads"). All purchase transactions are processed directly and securely by the platform app stores (**Google Play In-App Billing** for Android and **Apple In-App Purchases** for iOS/macOS). We **never** collect, receive, or store your credit card numbers, bank account numbers, or billing addresses. We use **RevenueCat** as our in-app purchase and entitlement infrastructure to validate purchase receipts, manage entitlement status, and restore purchases across your devices. RevenueCat processes anonymous device identifiers and purchase tokens in compliance with applicable privacy regulations.
-   **Usage Data & Analytics**: We use **Firebase Analytics** to collect anonymous telemetry data about your interactions with the App (e.g., game launch, floor reach, session length, wild mode breakthrough, combat events, IAP paywall interactions) to help us improve game balance and performance.
-   **Crash Reports & Diagnostics**: We use **Firebase Crashlytics** to collect anonymized crash logs, stack traces, and system diagnostic details to identify and fix technical issues.
-   **Device & Advertising Information**: We collect device-specific information (e.g., device model, operating system version). We use your device's Advertising Identifier (IDFA / GAID) to serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements via **Google AdMob**, including optional rewarded videos for in-game supply airdrops.

**3. Permissions**
-   **Internet & Network Access**: Required to process in-app purchase receipts via RevenueCat/Google Play, fetch advertising content via Google AdMob, and transmit anonymous analytics/crash diagnostic events to Firebase.
-   **Vibration / Haptics**: Used to provide tactile feedback during gameplay interactions.
-   **Audio Output**: Used to play in-game music and sound effects locally.

---

### How We Use Your Information

-   **In-App Purchase Fulfillment**: To deliver your purchased products (e.g., granting permanent ad-free status), validate receipts, and restore your digital purchases across compatible devices.
-   **Service Improvement**: To analyze game telemetry and usage trends via Firebase Analytics to improve performance and level balancing.
-   **Stability & Bug Fixing**: To monitor application crash reports via Firebase Crashlytics and resolve technical bugs.
-   **Advertising**: To deliver relevant advertisements and optional rewarded supplies via Google AdMob (for users who have not removed advertisements).

---

### Account Deletion and Data Retention

Since kuntower stores game save data locally on your device:
-   **Data Removal**: You can reset or erase your game progress at any time by clearing the App's data in your device settings or uninstalling the App.
-   **Purchases**: Digital in-app purchases remain associated with your Google Play or Apple account and can be restored using the "Restore Purchases" button in the App.

---

### Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:
-   [Google Privacy Policy (Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, AdMob, Google Play Billing & Games Services)](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
-   [RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy)
-   [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

---

### Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: **xiaojiangagaga@gmail.com**
