# Privacy Policy for picpic (PicPic AI Photo Cleaner)

**Last Updated: 2026-09-16**

This Privacy Policy describes how picpic ("PicPic", "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and discloses your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

---

### Privacy First: 100% On-Device Local Processing

PicPic is designed with user privacy as its core principle:
- **Zero Cloud Photo Uploads**: All photo scanning, AI quality scoring, blur detection, face/expression analysis, and duplicate/similar photo clustering (perceptual hashing & OpenCV feature matching) are executed **100% locally on your device**.
- **No External Photo Storage**: Your photos, video thumbnails, and album metadata are **never** uploaded, transmitted, or hosted on our or any external servers.

---

### Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

**1. Photo Library Access & Local Data**
- **Photo Library Permission**: Required strictly to display your device's photo gallery, run local AI photo quality evaluations, detect duplicate/similar photos, and manage the local safe trash bin or favorite protection lists.
- **Local App Data**: Analysis caches, local trash bin vault records (`picpic_trash_vault`), and user settings are stored **locally on your device**. Uninstalling the App or clearing local data completely removes all local App records.

**2. Information Collected Automatically**
- **In-App Purchases & Financial Data**: We offer optional in-app purchases (such as "PicPic Pro" or "Remove Ads"). All purchase transactions are processed directly and securely by official platform app stores (**Apple App Store** for iOS/macOS and **Google Play Store** for Android). We **never** collect, receive, or store your credit card numbers, bank account numbers, or billing addresses. We use **RevenueCat** as our in-app purchase and entitlement infrastructure to validate purchase receipts, manage entitlement status, and restore purchases across your devices.
- **Usage Data & Analytics**: We use **Firebase Analytics** to collect anonymous telemetry data about your interactions with the App (e.g., app launch, scan start, scan completion, paywall interactions) to help us improve performance and stability.
- **Crash Reports & Diagnostics**: We use **Firebase Crashlytics** to collect anonymized crash logs, stack traces, and system diagnostic details to identify and fix technical issues.
- **Device & Advertising Information**: We collect device-specific information (e.g., device model, OS version). We use your device's Advertising Identifier (IDFA / GAID) to serve advertisements via **Google AdMob** for users on the free tier.

**3. Permissions**
- **Photo Gallery Permission (`READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` / PhotoKit Access)**: Required to load and process local photos for AI quality analysis and cleanup.
- **Internet & Network Access**: Required to process in-app purchase receipts via RevenueCat / Apple / Google Play, fetch advertising content via Google AdMob, and transmit anonymous analytics/crash diagnostic events to Firebase.
- **Vibration / Haptics**: Used to provide tactile feedback during card swipes and cleanup actions.

---

### How We Use Your Information

- **Local Photo Management & Cleanup**: To perform local AI photo scoring, group duplicate photos, and manage your local safe trash bin.
- **In-App Purchase Fulfillment**: To deliver your purchased products (e.g., granting Pro features or permanent ad-free status), validate receipts, and restore your digital purchases across compatible devices.
- **Service Improvement**: To analyze anonymous app usage trends via Firebase Analytics to optimize scanning speed and app stability.
- **Stability & Bug Fixing**: To monitor application crash reports via Firebase Crashlytics and resolve technical bugs.
- **Advertising**: To deliver advertisements via Google AdMob for free-tier users.

---

### Account Deletion and Data Retention

Since PicPic stores photo analysis caches and trash records locally on your device:
- **Data Removal**: You can erase all local App caches and trash records at any time by clearing the App's storage in your device settings or uninstalling the App.
- **Purchases**: Digital in-app purchases remain associated with your Apple ID or Google Play account and can be restored using the "Restore Purchases" button in the App.

---

### Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:
- [Google Privacy Policy (Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, AdMob, Google Play)](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy)
- [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

---

### Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: **xiaojiangagaga@gmail.com**
