<a name="english"></a>
## 1. English (Default)

This Privacy Policy describes how **PicPic (PicLite)** (package identifier: `com.jannix.picpic`) handles your information.

### Core Principle: 100% On-Device Local Processing
- **Zero Cloud Photo Uploads**: All photo scanning, AI quality scoring, blur detection, face analysis, and duplicate/similar photo clustering run **100% locally on your device**.
- **No Remote Photo Storage**: Your photos and album metadata are **never** uploaded, transmitted, or hosted on any cloud server.

### Permissions & Data Usage
- **Photo Library Permission (`READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` / PhotoKit)**: Used strictly to read photos locally for gallery display, AI quality evaluation, blur filtering, and duplicate removal.
- **In-App Purchases**: Handled securely by official platform app stores (**Apple App Store** / **Google Play Store**). We use RevenueCat to validate entitlement status without collecting payment numbers.
- **Analytics & Crash Diagnostics**: Anonymous telemetry and crash logs are collected via **Firebase Analytics** and **Firebase Crashlytics** solely for bug fixes and stability improvements.
- **Advertising**: Served via **Google AdMob** for free-tier users.

### Data Retention & Removal
- All photo analysis caches and trash records are stored on your local device. Clearing local app cache or uninstalling the app permanently removes all local data.
